GeoHand

Open up the Earth with your bare hands and learn what each layer is. Your camera stays in the browser, nothing is uploaded.

Mouse: click a layer to highlight it, drag it to pull it out, drag empty space to rotate, scroll to zoom. Arrow keys step through the layers. Double-click or press R to reset.
