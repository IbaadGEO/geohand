# GeoHand sources: Earth's interior

Every entry below was checked against its DOI record on 2026-11-22: authors, year, title, journal, volume and pages. That confirms each paper exists and the citation is right. It does **not** confirm the values in it. Those are checked against the paper itself before a reference is marked `checked`.

Mark the **Access** column Y or N. "Open" means the paper is free to read without a subscription.
